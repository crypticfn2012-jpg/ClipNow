## What is ClipNow?

ClipNow is a community-built platform made specifically for **BuildNow.gg clips**.

Upload your best plays, discover other creators, build your profile, and keep your clips in one place without the usual generic gaming-platform stuff.

## Features

- **BuildNow.gg clips** — upload and share your gameplay clips
- **Automatic thumbnails** — clips can generate a video thumbnail automatically
- **Custom thumbnails** — use your own image when you want more control
- **Creator profiles** — display names, usernames, bios and profile pictures
- **Social links** — add YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch, Discord, GitHub, Kick and one custom link
- **Followers** — follow creators and see who follows you
- **Likes and comments** — interact with clips and creators
- **Search and discovery** — find clips and creators
- **Notifications and inbox** — keep up with follows, likes and comments
- **OG profiles** — special animated gold styling and an OG badge for selected early members
- **Moderation** — built-in checks help keep usernames, bios and uploaded content safer
- **Responsive design** — works across desktop and mobile

## Built for the community

ClipNow is not trying to be another massive social network.

It's a focused place for people who play **BuildNow.gg** and want somewhere made around their clips, creators and community.

## Tech

ClipNow is built with a lightweight web stack:

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Supabase
- GitHub Pages

The site is intentionally simple and client-focused, while Supabase handles authentication, profiles, storage and community data.

## Upload limits

The current ClipNow upload limit is **50 MB per clip** on the supported Supabase setup.

## Project status

ClipNow is actively being developed. Features and UI are still evolving as new ideas are added and existing systems are improved.
